# HRMS Payroll Period Close Checklist

## Pre-close checks
- Confirm the payroll period and employee population.
- Review approved attendance, leave, and compensation changes.
- Identify missing or duplicate payroll inputs.
- Reconcile gross pay, deductions, adjustments, and net pay.

## Close controls
Require an authorized reviewer before finalization. Prevent accidental edits to a closed period and define a controlled correction process.

## Evidence
Retain the period identifier, calculation version, approval record, reconciliation outcome, and any approved exceptions. Avoid placing full salary details in general-purpose logs.
