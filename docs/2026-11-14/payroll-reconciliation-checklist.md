# Payroll Reconciliation Checklist

> Created: 2026-11-14

Plan reconciliation of gross pay, allowances, deductions, unpaid leave, net pay, payslip totals, and payroll exports. Include rounding, employee eligibility, period cutoffs, duplicate processing, and traceable correction records.
